# Product requirements

## Problem and target users
State the problem, evidence, assumptions and affected users.

## Goals and non-goals
Define intended outcomes, success measures and explicitly excluded work.

## Requirements
Use stable identifiers such as REQ-001. For each, record behavior, priority, rationale and source.
Include errors, accessibility, privacy and performance requirements where relevant.

## Acceptance criteria
Write observable Given/When/Then cases linked to requirement IDs; define failure behavior.

## Open questions and change control
Mark unresolved assumptions. Identify who can accept risk or approve scope changes.
